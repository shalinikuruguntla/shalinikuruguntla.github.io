SafePath

MSc dissertation · team of five · 11 sprints

A live route planner for London that ranks walking routes by how safe they are, not only how short. We surveyed 53 people (78% put safety before speed) and ran usability tests before launch.

  • Built the pipelines that clean Met Police crime data (dropping records with missing coordinates or crime types, filtering to London) and load it into a 1km grid in PostGIS.
  • Designed the scoring: weighted 14 crime types by severity, ranked grid cells by percentile to find hotspots, and combined crime, collisions, lighting and hazards with weights users can adjust.
  • Built the APIs and their security: JWT authentication, password hashing and input validation.
  • Built the map views (React-Leaflet), including a safest-vs-fastest comparison and live hazard alerts over WebSockets.

The NC6 filing robot

Every solar installation has to be registered with the network operator using an NC6 form on the operator's website, so we automated the filing.

  1. 01Look upFinds the grant details for the installation.
  2. 02Fill inDrives a real browser with Playwright to complete the operator's online form.
  3. 03File & attachDownloads the filed PDF and attaches it to the deal in the CRM.
  4. 04Ask when neededSends a question to a person only when it can't continue on its own.

Runs on its own server so it never slows the CRM down, and talks to the CRM over a private, signed link.

Screens aren't shown, to protect client and customer data under GDPR.
